## Title: Quality Control Verification of Clinical Scales and Equipment Constancy
* **Applies To:** Nuclear Medicine and Clinical Support Staff
* **Regulations Met:** IRR17, IR(ME)R 2017, and Clinical Governance Protocols

---

### Purpose & Scope
This Standard Operating Procedure defines the mandatory operational sequences for completing daily and weekly scale constancy verifications, glucometer Quality Control checks, and environmental tracking within the Nuclear Medicine department. Regular equipment verification ensures optimum diagnostic accuracy, maintains compliance with statutory safety regulations, and mitigates the risk of clinical calculation errors.

### Personnel Responsibilities
* **Authorized Staff:** Trained Band 3 Clinical Support Workers / Imaging Assistants are authorized to execute physical checks, record primary data metrics, and flag immediate variances.
* **Escalation Staff:** The Lead Nuclear Medicine Technologist or Departmental Physicist is responsible for reviewing outliers and executing corrective calibrations.

---

### Step-by-Step Equipment Verification Procedures
Patient weight values directly dictate radiopharmaceutical dose calculations and timed plasma GFR sampling metrics. Scale verification must be performed daily prior to patient registration and check-in.

#### 1. Clinical Patient Scales: Constancy Verification Sequence
1. **Visual Inspection:** Confirm the scale platform is level, clean, and entirely clear of external contact or debris.
2. **Initialisation:** Power on the digital scale unit and verify the digital display reads exactly `0.0 kg`.
3. **Standard Weight Application:** Place a certified, calibrated 10.0 kg reference weight precisely onto the centre of the scale platform.
4. **Metric Recording:** Allow the digital reading to stabilize. Record the final display reading on the Departmental Environmental Spreadsheet.
5. **Variance Tolerance Evaluation:**
   * *Pass:* If the reading falls between 9.9 kg and 10.1 kg (Variance within +/- 1%), the equipment is verified for safe clinical use.
   * *Fail:* If the reading falls outside this range, the scale must be immediately labelled "OUT OF SERVICE", removed from the clinical area, and escalated to Medical Engineering.

#### 2. Blood Glucose POCT (Point-of-Care Testing) Quality Control (QC) Sequence
Blood Glucose POCT devices must be verified every 24 hours to ensure accurate patient capillary blood glucose assessments. Failure to perform this QC will result in the device rejecting any attempt at running glucose measurements.
1. **Sanitization:** Clean the outer chassis of the POCT blood glucose meter using an approved clinic-grade wipe.
2. **Strip Insertion:** Insert a fresh, unexpired glucose test strip into the primary port; verify that the device automatically powers on and displays the correct batch code.
3. **Control Solution Delivery:** Agitate the Level 1 (Low) Control Solution vial thoroughly. Apply a single droplet to the target zone of the test strip.
4. **Verification & Repeat:** Confirm the device processes the reading within the automated window. Compare the digital result against the acceptable target range printed on the control solution packaging. Repeat the entire process using Level 2 (High) Control Solution.
5. **Digital Compliance Entry:** Log the completed QC confirmation directly on the electronic interface to unblock the device for patient testing.

#### 3. Environmental and Cold-Chain Infrastructure Tracking
* **Temperature Checks:** Document ambient temperatures daily across injection bays, scan rooms, and patient waiting zones. Ambient temperatures must remain stable between 19°C and 23°C to ensure optimal equipment functionality.
* **Cold-Chain Refrigerator Logs:** Verify and record the internal temperature of pharmaceutical storage refrigerators. The reading must strictly reside within the `+2°C` to `+8°C` regulatory threshold.
* **Urgent Escalation Pathway:** Any infrastructure temperature deviation persisting for more than 45 minutes must be instantly escalated to the Estates Department to prevent the degradation of temperature-sensitive clinical stocks.

---

### Data Integrity & Documentation Archive
All completed verification parameters must be recorded immediately and contemporaneously. Physical paperwork logs must be scanned systematically and archived digitally within the department’s RIS (Radiology Information System) compliance portal. In accordance with IRR17 and clinical governance regulations, all equipment verification logs must be safely retained in the digital archive for a minimum duration of 5 years.
